SMALL MODEL. VISIBLE REASONS.

A suggestion,
with the receipts.

  1. Match the meaning. A pinned, open-weight MiniLM model embeds both sets locally in your browser. Model files are about 23 MB, plus the runtime.
  2. Check the details. Lexical guards look for changes to quantities, dates, negation, selected status words and uppercase identifiers. Close or reused matches go to review.
  3. Keep the originals. Every current note and its earlier reference remain visible. The audit includes text, scores, reasons, settings and measured timings.
Know the boundary. The model reads at most 256 tokens per note. The guards are incomplete and can also flag harmless rewrites. This is an English prototype for short notes, not a factual verifier or an approval tool. Test with non-sensitive text; your hosted copy still trusts its host and your browser. No application upload endpoint, external model request, telemetry or persistent note storage is included.
